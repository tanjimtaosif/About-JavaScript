Q1: Stop at First Multiple of 7
Write a loop from 1 to 100 that:
- Prints each number
- Stops completely when it finds the first number divisible by 7

Q2: Skip Multiples of 3
Write a loop from 1 to 20 that: 
Skips numbers divisible by 3 Prints all others
- Use continue
- Expected output:
  1 2 4 5 7 8 10 11 ... (no 3, 6, 9, etc.)

Q3: Print First 5 0dd Numbers Only
Write a loop from 1 to 100 that:
Prints only 5 odd numbers Then stops the loop
- Use both if, continue, and a counter + break
- Expected output:
  1 3 5 7 9
