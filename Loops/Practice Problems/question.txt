1. Very Basic Loop Warm-ups (Numbers)
   a. Print numbers from 1 to 10
   b. Print numbers from 10 to 1
   c. Print all even numbers between 1 and 50
   d. Print all odd numbers between 1 and 50
   e. Print the multiplication table of 5
   f. Print the squares of numbers from 1 to 10

2. Loop + Conditions
    a. Print numbers from 1 to 100
        1. Print "Fizz" if divisible by 3
        2. Print "Buzz" if divisible by 5
        3. Print "FizzBuzz" if divisible by both
    b. Count how many numbers between 1 and 100 are divisible by 7
    c. Find the sum of numbers from 1 to 100
    d. Find the sum of only even numbers between 1 and 100
